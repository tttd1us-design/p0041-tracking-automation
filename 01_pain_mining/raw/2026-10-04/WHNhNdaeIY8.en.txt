Mr. Lee, an employee at a clothing store in a large shopping mall, made
75 million won in card payments over an eight-month period starting in July 2018.
The place where the payments were made was the store he himself owned. This is a case of so-
called "fake sales," inflating the figures to make it appear as if a lot of goods had been sold.
Why were the employees forced to do this? The shopping
mall, which takes a commission from the bills of the tenants' sales, pressures the tenants to increase their
sales. Unable to withstand this pressure, the store manager pressures the employees again to make card payments, thereby artificially inflating sales. We
obtained and verified the employees' card payment records. Payments
of 710,000 won were made 24 times, and 17 million won was paid in a single day. On three occasions,
1 million won was partially canceled.
This practice of fake sales continued for three years starting in 2016. When the
issue surfaced last year, the shopping mall refused to cancel the payments, claiming it was the personal misconduct of the clothing store manager. The
employees' account is different. They trembled under pressure to make payments, with shopping mall employees visiting and demanding cards whenever sales dropped, and warnings that they would be dragged away by the
chairman if sales declined.
